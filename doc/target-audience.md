# Dragochi Target Audience

## Positioning Rule

Dragochi is **female-first but inclusive**. We design first for women who use games and journaling as emotional self-care, while welcoming anyone with the same behavioral needs.

## Primary Persona 1: Emotional Logger

### Traits

- Loves aesthetic and expressive interfaces.
- Enjoys recording small daily details and feelings.
- Treats game moments as part of personal life memory.
- Shares daily snippets in IG Story-like formats.

### Core jobs-to-be-done

- "Help me capture my emotional state quickly without friction."
- "Help me remember meaningful micro-moments from today."
- "Help me organize game context and life context together."

### Triggers

- Finishing a game session and wanting to "save the feeling."
- A mood shift during or after play.
- End-of-day reflection with low mental energy.

### Success outcomes

- Entries feel personal, cute, and easy to revisit.
- Emotional patterns become understandable over time.
- Logging feels comforting rather than task-heavy.

### Anti-goals

- Feeling judged by performance-first metrics.
- Being forced into long-form writing every time.
- A cold utility tone that ignores emotional context.

## Primary Persona 2: Self-Healing Gamer

### Traits

- Uses gaming as decompression and self-regulation.
- Wants gentle structure, not strict productivity pressure.
- Values calm and companion-like product language.
- Prefers reflection that supports recovery and reset.

### Core jobs-to-be-done

- "Help me understand if gaming helped my mood today."
- "Help me build a healthy rhythm with soft prompts."
- "Help me feel accompanied when I log my day."

### Triggers

- Stress, burnout, social fatigue, or overthinking.
- Needing a low-pressure ritual after a long day.
- Looking back to find what play patterns felt healing.

### Success outcomes

- Can identify personal "comfort play" patterns.
- Feels emotionally seen by the app's copy and structure.
- Maintains a sustainable logging habit without guilt.

### Anti-goals

- Competitive or grind-heavy framing as the default.
- Reward systems that punish missed days.
- Overly noisy UI that overwhelms mood tracking.

## Copy Guardrails

### Do

- Use warm, calm, supportive language.
- Frame actions as invitations, not commands.
- Keep wording emotionally safe and non-judgmental.
- Prefer companion language: "reflect," "check in," "capture this moment."

### Don't

- Use grind/productivity-pressure framing.
- Use aggressive performance language as default voice.
- Reduce user value to duration totals or efficiency alone.
- Use emotionally distant, purely mechanical labels.

## Tone Phrase Samples

### Good examples

- "Capture this moment"
- "How did this session feel?"
- "A gentle check-in before you close today"
- "Your day, your pace"

### Avoid examples

- "Ready to grind"
- "Maximize your output"
- "Beat yesterday's hours"
- "No excuses, keep streaking"
